Your Intercom replies, written in one click.

Support Copilot is a free Chrome extension for support agents. Open a customer conversation in Intercom, pick what you need, and it drafts the reply from your own help center articles.

  1. Open a conversation in Intercom.
  2. Click the extension and pick what you need: a chat or email reply, a handoff note, or an escalation for your team.
  3. Review and send. Replies appear in Intercom's reply box. Escalations stay in the extension, so a customer never sees them.

Three files. No server.

Plain JavaScript on Chrome Manifest V3. Your API key stays in local Chrome storage and calls go straight to Anthropic.

What happensWhere
Read the threadWalks Intercom's DOM, shadow roots included, and labels every message as customer, agent, bot or system.content.js
Check the help centerKeywords from the latest customer message query your KB. The top three articles become the only allowed source.background.js
Draft and analyse at onceTwo Claude Haiku 4.5 calls run through Promise.all, so the analysis adds no wait. About two seconds.background.js
Land it on the right sideReplies are pasted into the composer for review. Escalations stay in the popup's copy panel.popup.js
  • About a centper conversation on Haiku 4.5
  • Survives redesignsevery selector has a fallback chain

Running in three minutes.

Open source and free to fork. You bring your own Claude API key.

  1. Get a Claude API keyCreate one at console.anthropic.com. A $5 credit covers hundreds of conversations.
  2. Load the extensionOpen chrome://extensions, turn on Developer mode, click Load unpacked and pick the folder.
  3. Fill in SettingsYour key, name and company. Add a help center search URL and your team's tone rules if you have them.
  4. Open a conversationTick a request type. Review, edit, send.
